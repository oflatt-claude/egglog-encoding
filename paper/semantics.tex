  \end{minipage}
  \caption{Egglog representation. Terms are finite trees without variables, including $f()$.
    $\Sigma$ maps names to declarations, $E$ stores
    asserted equalities, and $\mathcal R$ stores rules.
    A function row separates key and output terms with a semicolon and is stored as $(r,r)$.
    $\operatorname{Rows}(D)$ selects these rows from $E$ using their declarations;
    it is not additional storage. Adding $(t,t)$ asserts the existence of $t$; $T_E$ collects stored subterms.}
  \label{fig:representation}
